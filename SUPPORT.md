# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/Shades-Media-Group/ImageRefold/discussions).
- **An image came out wrong:** open an [export report](https://github.com/Shades-Media-Group/ImageRefold/issues/new?template=export.yml) with the settings you used.
- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Shades-Media-Group/ImageRefold/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
- **A security issue:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

ImageRefold is free and maintained by [Shades Media Group](https://shades.md). Answers come from the maintainers and the community, usually within a few days.
