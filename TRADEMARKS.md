ImageRefold is open source, but its name and logo are not.

[LICENSE](LICENSE) covers everything that is code: the plugin, the website's source and the scripts. You may use, copy, change and sell work built with them, with or without credit.

## What it does not cover

These belong to **Shades Media Group** and are not licensed for reuse:

- **The names** "ImageRefold", "Shades" and "Shades Media Group" as names of a business, product or service.
- **The ImageRefold logo**: every file in `assets/brand/` and every image of the mark or the lockup.
- **The Shades mark**, which the website shows as the maker's credit.

## You may

- Link to ImageRefold, or show its logo next to a link to it, without changing the logo.
- Write about ImageRefold in an article or review.
- Fork the code and ship your own plugin under your own name and logo.

## Please don't

- Publish a fork under the ImageRefold name or logo, or one that looks like it comes from Shades.
- Make a modified version of the logo.
