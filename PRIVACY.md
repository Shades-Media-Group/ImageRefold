# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

ImageRefold is a Figma plugin made by Shades Media Group. This policy covers the plugin and its website, imagerefold.shades.md.

## The short version

ImageRefold does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Your images never leave your computer.

## What the Figma plugin accesses

- **The layers you select**, to export them as images and encode them in the format you choose.
- **Images you drop or open in the plugin**, to compress them and place them in the current Figma file.
- **Figma's plugin storage**, to remember your settings and presets.

The plugin is built with no network access: its manifest allows no domains, so Figma blocks any request. Encoding runs inside the plugin window, on your machine.

## The website

The website is static. It sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. A display preference you choose stays in your browser.

## Data we receive

None. There are no accounts, analytics, crash reports or tracking of any kind.

## Children

ImageRefold is a design tool and is not directed at children.
